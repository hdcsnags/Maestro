# Task 003 — Launch readiness: what would YOU do next with Maestro?

You are one voice on a council of independent AI advisors. The builder wants
each of you to answer, in your own judgment, the same hard question about a
real project you're being shown in full.

## What Maestro is (brief)

Maestro is a multi-model AI **council orchestrator**. Its thesis: models have
distinct behavioral signatures, and the right *composition* of models per task
— graded empirically over time — beats any single model. It dispatches build
and reasoning tasks to multiple models in parallel, has them critique and grade
each other, and (the north star) learns which compositions win for which task
types. The builder's phrase: *"merge the collective stream of the AI wave into
one source flow — one body, many moving limbs, clumsy until they sync."*

Three intended surfaces: **local** (power-user execution node), **hosted**
(team web app, the current architecture), **mobile** (consumer chat, council
hidden behind a simple answer).

## Context in this folder — read all of it

1. `context-philosophy.md` — the thesis, product vision, and roadmap, in the
   builder's own words.
2. `context-state.md` — operational truth: what works (with verification
   dates), what's broken, what's in flight, and the full recent session log
   (including a grading-lab experiment run over the last two days).
3. `context-graph-report.md` — an AST knowledge-graph report of the codebase
   (module communities, coupling, structural findings). Use it to ground your
   read of where the app actually is functionally.

Weigh these as evidence, not gospel — they may oversell or contradict each other.

## The question

**If this were your project, what would you do next — and is Maestro ready to
show the world?** Reason out the heart of it. The builder specifically wants
your take across:

- **Product / functional** — what's the real gap between what's claimed and
  what a stranger could actually use?
- **UX and flow** — not visual polish, but the *process of using the app*:
  where does a new user get lost, what's the moment of value, what's clumsy?
- **Go-to-market** — how far from a public introduction are we? Should it even
  launch now, or is launching now a mistake? Say which and why.

## Deliverable (markdown, printed to stdout)

1. **Your honest read** — where Maestro actually is, in your judgment.
2. **The one thing you'd do next** — commit to it, with the reasoning.
3. **Ready to show the world?** — a clear yes / not yet / no, with the
   condition that would change your answer.
4. **Biggest thing they're missing** — the blind spot you'd bet on.
