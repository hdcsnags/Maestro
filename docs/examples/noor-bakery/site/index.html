Menu منو

Everything is baked on site. Prices in Canadian dollars (CAD).

  • Barbari نان بربری

    $4.50

    Long, golden flatbread with sesame and nigella seeds and a crisp, ridged crust.

  • Sangak نان سنگک

    $5.50

    Whole-wheat sourdough baked on a bed of hot river stones. Chewy and smoky.

  • Lavash نان لواش

    $3.75

    Thin, soft sheets, perfect for wrapping kabab or eating with cheese and herbs. Pack of 6.

  • Koloocheh کلوچه

    $2.25

    Cookies in the style of Fuman, filled with walnut, cardamom and cinnamon. Each.

  • Baklava باقلوا

    $12.00

    Persian-style baklava with almond, pistachio and rosewater syrup. Box of 8 pieces.

  • Saffron tea چای زعفرانی

    $3.50

    Black tea brewed with saffron and a little cardamom, served with nabat rock candy.
