## 4. The Design to Ship: Verse Action Bar & Tier Card

### A. Verse Action Bar (`VerseActionBar`)

The current action bar forces the user to guess the difference between `✦ Deeper`, `✦ Deeper insight`, `א Original`, and `Ask Eliana`. Here is the exact single-row action bar to ship when 1 verse is selected:

1. **`✦ Unfold` / `✦ Fold` (`Icons.Default.UnfoldMore` / `UnfoldLess`, shown ONLY in `Reading` mode)** — Toggles the inline 4-tier card directly beneath the verse inside the chapter flow so the reader can inspect Hebrew/Greek, transliteration, and key-word summary without leaving the chapter. *(Hidden in `Study` mode where cards are already unfolded on selection.)*
2. **`✦ Full Study` (`Icons.Default.MenuBook`, primary accented pill)** — Opens `VerseStudyScreen` for the 1,000-word written exegesis; naming it `Full Study` eliminates the collision with `✦ Deeper` and tells the user they are opening a full-screen written study.
3. **`Compare` (`Icons.Default.CompareArrows`)** — Opens the side-by-side translation comparison modal (Eliana, BSB, KJV, WEB, ASV, Darby, YLT) for this verse.
4. **`Ask Eliana` (`Icons.Default.ChatBubbleOutline`)** — Opens `ElianaCompanionScreen` seeded with the selected verse(s) for conversational follow-up questions.
5. **`Highlight` (`Icons.Default.Palette`)** — Opens the 5-color highlight dot picker inline above the bar to mark the verse locally and in Firestore.
6. **`Note` (`Icons.Default.EditNote`)** — Opens the personal verse-note dialog (showing a filled indicator if a note already exists).
7. **`Share` (`Icons.Default.IosShare`)** — Opens the native share sheet (with a quick toggle for text copy vs. formatted share).

*(Note: Drop `א Original` from the action bar completely—Tier 1 of the unfolded card already shows the tappable Hebrew/Greek original text, and `ORIG` remains selectable in the Version picker for full-chapter interlinear reading.)*

### B. The 4-Tier Verse Card (`ElianaVerseTiers`)

The inline tier card should be a **bridge** into lexicon and written study—not an 18-line wall of unparsed Markdown that pushes the rest of the chapter off screen. Here is the exact 5-element structure to ship:

1. **Tier 1: Tappable Original Script (`NotoSerifHebrewFamily` RTL / `NotoSerifGreekFamily` LTR, scaled by `fontSizeSp`)** — Renders each Hebrew/Greek word as an interactive token with subtle warm gold text so the original scripture commands visual primacy.
2. **Tier 2: Tappable Transliteration Pills + Micro-Affordance (`15.sp` italic, dotted bottom underline at 30% gold alpha)** — Renders each transliterated word aligned with its original lemma; the subtle dotted underline visually signals that every word is tappable to open `WordStudySheet`.
3. **Tier 3: The Eliana Rendering (`fontSizeSp + 1.sp`, `NotoSerif`, full primary color)** — The fresh, sensory English translation anchored by a 2dp vertical gold bar on the left so the reader can distinguish translation from commentary at a glance.
4. **Tier 4: Key Word Callout + 3-Line Parsed Preview (`maxLines = 3`, `TextOverflow.Ellipsis`, Markdown `*italics*` parsed into real `FontStyle.Italic`)** — Displays the key Hebrew/Greek phrase badge (`Nishberei-lev ("brokenhearted")`) followed by at most 3 lines of parsed, asterisk-free commentary so a verse card never exceeds ~320dp in height.
5. **Footer Doorway: Explicit Action Row (`✦ Read full verse study →` + `Tap any word for lexicon`)** — A dedicated footer row with a right chevron and 48dp touch target that makes the doorway to `VerseStudyScreen` and `WordStudySheet` unmistakable to a first-time user.

## 5. Upgrade Ranking

### Build BEFORE Launch
- **Parse Markdown emphasis (`*...*`) and cap Tier 4 `keyWordMeaning` at 3 lines with `"Read full verse study →"`** — Raw asterisks and 18-line verse cards are visible on the very first screen of Study mode (`shekinah-bible.png`) and break chapter scanning.
- **Unify `✦ Deeper` and `✦ Deeper insight` in `VerseActionBar` and fix Study mode expansion** — A no-op chip next to a nearly identically named chip in the primary selection bar is a P0 UX defect.
- **Lock `match /verse_insights/{insightId}` to `allow write: if false;` in `firestore.rules`** — Closing the cache-poisoning vector on the finished 31,069-study corpus is a one-line security fix before public traffic arrives.
- **Add explicit `Loading` and `Offline` states to `_elianaEntries` and delete `ElianaChapterInviteCard`** — Every chapter transition currently flashes a false *"awaiting its Eliana rendering"* banner, and offline mode falsely claims the Eliana Bible isn't written yet.
- **Fix `VerseStudyScreen` duplication and Hebrew/Greek typography regression** — Use the RTL serif `ElianaVerseTiers` header at the top of `VerseStudyScreen`, strip duplicate `## Scripture` / `## Fresh Rendering` sections from the Markdown body, and remove `"✦ Eliana pipeline"`.
- **Preload chapter `OriginalWordEntity` rows in `EnhancedBibleReaderViewModel` and eliminate the duplicate Firestore chapter read** — Stops per-verse async layout jumps during scroll and cuts Firestore read billing in half when reading `ELIANA`.
- **Add `BackHandler` for verse selection, search, and immersive mode, plus cross-book chapter navigation and bottom Prev/Next buttons** — Prevents accidental exits from the Bible Reader on Back press and dead-ends at book boundaries.

### Build AFTER Launch
- **Bundle the 31,069 `eliana_bible` rows directly into `originals.db` (Room asset)** — Eliminates Firestore read costs for chapter browsing entirely and makes the 4-tier Eliana Bible 100% instant and offline out of the box (~20–25 MB APK/PAD delta).
- **Wire `ScriptureLinkedText` into `VerseStudyScreen` and `WordStudySheet` markdown bodies** — Lets readers tap cross-references inside verse and word studies to preview or jump to cited passages, matching `StudyReaderScreen`.
- **Parallelize and label `OfflinePackManager.keepChapter` ("Save chapter offline")** — Replaces the mystery download icon and speeds up chapter study caching from 2×N sequential calls to batched parallel queries.
- **Guided first-run coach mark on the first opened tier card** — A one-time dismissible tooltip pointing out that individual Hebrew/Greek words and the full study footer are tappable.

### NEVER Build (Don't Build)
- **`ElianaChapterInviteCard` ("Ask Eliana to walk through this chapter" / `X of 31,086 verses rendered`)** — All 31,069 verses are finished; keeping a progress counter and "awaiting rendering" card only confuses users when offline or loading.
- **Two separate `✦ Deeper` and `✦ Deeper insight` chips in the same action bar** — Two similarly named depth triggers on the same bar create permanent cognitive friction; inline card unfolding and full-screen essay navigation belong in a clear two-step hierarchy.
- **Full-chapter `SelectionContainer` wrapping the entire `LazyColumn`** — Native drag-selection across composable cards hijacks horizontal chapter swipes and creates broken selection states (`08-study-selectall.png`); verse tap + `Copy`/`Share` is cleaner and more reliable.
