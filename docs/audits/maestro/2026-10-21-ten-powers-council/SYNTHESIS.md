## 2. Contested (axis named)

- **Q3 — Debate Room design.** All three partial. Convergent amendments, adopted into the design: a **fixed** dissent seat (not rotating; rotation forces stance changes that fight stability-stopping), **cap at two rounds**, **exclude the dissent seat from the convergence score**, **drop the silence rule** (it inflates stability), **a stance change must cite new evidence**, **send checkable items to a verifier between rounds instead of another debate round**, **prompt only on contested items**, and **snapshot rounds** (seats read a compact per-round digest, not the whole transcript, so agy's response window holds). Sol adds item-level evidence stability as the stop signal. Axis left to the build: stop on item-level evidence stability (Sol) vs stance stability (proposal); default to Sol's.
- **Q6 — Michael's idea (non-developer use).** Sound in spirit, wrong in shape as first cut. Opus: the smallest real version is *her own CLIs plus a brainstorm brief and plain-language synthesis*, making her the first external CLI user. Gemini: cross-account web→Claw routing is blocked in code (`executor-api` poll/claim require `requested_by = executor.owner_user_id`; `broadcast()` filters maestroclaw agents), so ship a single-workspace `council_run` job first. Sol: a short *editable* intake plus a bounded read-only scoping council. Two risks named: sharing personal CLI subscriptions with another person may breach provider terms (Opus, unverified); the intake's private part `p` must never reach the client export (Sol, verified; the First Call page already hides it, the skill text is corrected). **Ruling: needs_owner** on the account model and the ToS question. The build proceeds with the web brainstorm intent first and the discovery intent as an editable record, both on Michael's own workspace.
- **Q7 — what is missing.** Opus (disagree): containment for untrusted repos (agy runs with skip-permissions; the read-only check is post-hoc), depth-1 local clones were full history (**fixed**: `file://` clone), no per-run cost cap, no council-vs-solo baseline, author-synthesizes-own-review (disclosed above). Sol: immutable replay inputs (**fixed**: resume reads the frozen `prompt.md`) and independent outcome evaluation. Gemini: a **Context Packer and Blindness Scrubber** power (`council pack`: a curated folder of exactly what seats may see; `--scrub` landed as the first half) and the CLI-subscription ToS risk. Adopted: scrub flag now, `council pack`, a `--max-seat-minutes` cap and a council-vs-solo baseline task in grading-lab queued as Power 1/4 follow-ups. Containment on Windows stays an owner decision (VM or container for the worker), as the attack plan said.
